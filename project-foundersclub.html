Project Background & The Mission

Founders Club is a student entrepreneurship hub dedicated to grooming future innovators and founders. The previous digital presence needed a complete overhaul to authentically reflect the club’s vibrancy, showcase proven alumni success stories, and engage prospective students during school outreach events.

Together with a friend, we redesigned and rebuilt the platform from the ground up, pairing modern community UI with an intelligent interactive assistant tailored for the upcoming Open House.

The Open House AI Twist: School Matcher & Pragmatic Ideator

Rather than just a static brochure, we introduced an interactive AI advisor designed to directly consult prospective students at the Open House booth. Powered by Groq’s ultrafast inference engine (leveraging the 14,000 daily free API requests), the AI gives students authentic, grounded startup coaching:

1

Aspiration & School Matcher

Prospects share their natural interests and career aspirations. The model recommends which academic school or diploma track best fits their trajectory.

2

The Twist: Grounded Idea Validation

When students pitch their dream business concept, the AI doesn't just praise them blindly. Instead, it challenges them with practical validation: "Not everything needs to be an app on day one."

3

Actionable Real-World Steps

It prescribes lean, immediate experiments before writing code. For example, if someone pitches a Study Buddy app, the AI advises: "Before building an app, call up classmates, exchange notes, and test if peers actually study together consistently."

💡 Why this matters: It teaches young aspiring founders the lean startup mindset early — validating real demand with human interactions first before spending months building software that nobody asked for.

Key Platform Pillars

Groq API Integration

Integrated Groq's high-throughput LPU inference API. Capitalized on the 14,000 daily requests free quota to deliver instantaneous, zero-lag responses without incurring operational costs for the student club.

Founder Testimonial Hub

Curated showcase highlighting authentic journeys of past club members and student entrepreneurs ("preneurs") who launched businesses, won hackathons, and accessed seed support.

Events & Workshops Radar

Clean event feed spotlighting upcoming networking nights, pitch clinics, founder fireside chats, and incubation workshops for active members and prospective recruits.

Warm Editorial & Kiosk UX

Crafted a warm, welcoming editorial design featuring an inviting cream-and-terracotta palette, bold headline typography ("Where NYP's Boldest Begin"), student collaboration photography, and an approachable interactive kiosk experience for Open House visitors.

Key Takeaways & Impact

  • Collaborative Pair Engineering: Redesigning from the ground up reinforced strong front-end conventions, component modularity, and smooth Git workflows between two developers.
  • Cost-Effective AI Architecture: Selected Groq strategically to harness near-instant latency and high-volume limits (14K req/day), creating an enterprise-grade experience on a student club budget.
  • Interactive Open House Engagement: Transformed what would have been a passive reading experience into an active, consultative touchpoint where prospects discover their future academic and entrepreneurial path.
